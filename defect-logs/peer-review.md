## Matching rule
Treat findings as the same defect when they identify the same underlying requirement problem, even if wording or IDs differ. Do not count two descriptions of the same root cause twice. Keep genuinely different defects separate.

## Formula
**Detection rate = |A ∩ B| ÷ |A ∪ B| × 100%**

The assignment describes this as “yours ÷ union”; the numerator must be the number of your findings that match the union, i.e. the intersection/common findings.
