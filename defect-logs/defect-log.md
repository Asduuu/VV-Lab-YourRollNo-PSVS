**Total findings:** 23 (including cross-requirement findings).  
**Status:** All findings are proposed/open pending review and approval.

## Cross-requirement findings
- **D08 (S4 vs S5):** ownership-based withdrawal conflicts with emergency release to any requesting party unless S5 is defined as a controlled exception.
- **D12 (S6 vs S7):** S6 repeats a broad temperature-safety statement while S7 gives a specific upper limit; S6 needs an additional measurable condition or should be consolidated.
- **D13 (S7 vs S8):** a 0°C maintenance temperature breaches the stated -18°C maximum unless a defined exception is introduced.
- **D19 (S5 vs S10):** the annual withdrawal quota does not say whether emergency releases count.
- **D21 (S5 vs S11):** redeposit rules do not clarify return of an emergency-released box.

## Inspection note
The log distinguishes defects in a single requirement from defects that arise only when requirements are considered together. The baseline wording remains unchanged; suggested fixes require a change request.
